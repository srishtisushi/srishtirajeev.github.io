Some projects I'm working on outside of my main research and academic commitments:

## modeling heterodimeric kinase activity

My undergraduate thesis involves developing a computational
package to estimate and predict heterodimeric kinase activity,
with the goal of capturing changes in specific molecular
interactions that gene-level differential expression analyses
may overlook.


## interpretability in EEG analysis

Currently using concept activation vectors to map out why CNNs predict/classify
certain EEG signals the way they do, but I hope to expand to other types of interpretability
frameworks later on. Running this both on [public data](https://openneuro.org/) and data collected from our [OpenBCI pipeline](https://github.com/UA-BCILab/ATCNet-Hand-Squeeze-Task), which uses ATCNet. 

## AI Consciousness, Organoid Sentience, and More

The race to AI Consciousness has never been faster. Working on several papers on the validity of the current arguments presented on digital minds and neurocomputing with my lab colleagues and PIs, Thomas Bever, Ph.D., and Brian Lord, Ph.D.

I'm also in the process of publishing some pretty exciting interviews we've had with various guest speakers in the frontiers of this space over the years, which has become more relevant now than ever. Stay tuned!
